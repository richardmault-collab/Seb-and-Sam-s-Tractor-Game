# Seb & Sam's Tractor Farm — v1.4 Development Scope

**Status:** Approved direction / implementation backlog (not yet implemented)  
**Baseline:** v1.3 Android game with two child profiles, eight activities and offline sound recordings.  
**Goal:** Replace shallow tap loops with purposeful, joyful farm play and substantially improve visual quality, usability and realism.

## Product principles

- **Sam (age 1):** Very large touch areas, cause-and-effect discovery, forgiving taps, no reading, no timed tasks. Where a drag is required for Seb, offer a tap-to-place equivalent for Sam.
- **Seb (age 3):** Short multi-step jobs, drag-and-drop, simple visual checklists, repetition with variation, count no higher than **5**.
- Friendly celebration without punishment, game-over states, timers, adverts, logins or network access.
- Real farm recordings for animals and machinery; speech is reserved for useful instructions, encouragement and counting.
- Screens, controls and illustrated characters should share a recognisable art direction. Aim for a polished game, not a collection of emoji buttons.
- Do **not** commit the original family reference photograph into the public GitHub repo. Include only approved, child-safe illustrated assets.

## A. Global visual redesign and navigation

- [ ] **Fix logo:** Replace layered, overlapping HTML/SVG text with a single cohesive, readable wooden sign or carefully spaced text-and-art composition. Verify 'Seb & Sam’s Tractor Farm' on narrow Android phones.
- [ ] **Home hero:** Replace the oversized/ambiguous tractor with a clearly recognisable tractor (engine bonnet, front grille, exhaust, cab/seat, different-size wheels and trailer hitch). Reduce the art's height so **both boys' heads and faces are unobstructed**.
- [ ] **Child characters:** Commission/export **separate transparent assets** for Seb and Sam, consistent with the reference photo and existing mockups. Seb: light brown bowl-cut hair, joyful grin, rust/orange top; Sam: younger proportions, very light blond hair, blue eyes, beige fleece/gilet. Keep the same character design across game screens, not inconsistent AI-generated variants.
- [ ] **Farm world:** Use layered countryside, fields, fences, hedges, barns, haystacks and small ambient motion. Replace generic emoji scenery where practical.
- [ ] **Buttons:** Give every activity an identifiable farm-object illustration, colour-coded card, oversized hit area and pressed/disabled feedback. The icon for **Guess the Farm Sound** must be farm-related (e.g. barn plus sound waves, or cow plus sound waves), not a generic speaker.
- [ ] **Game titles:** Consistent wooden-plank heading with an unobstructed back arrow. No clipping or text collisions.
- [ ] **Screen layout:** Verify on small Android screens in portrait: no inaccessible cards, obscured controls or horizontal scrolling. Consider a scrollable home menu with hero art that does not push the game list too far down.
- [ ] Sound/music toggles clearly show state and stay separate. Confirm no background music competes with sound-guessing clues.
- [ ] Shared animations: tractor exhaust puffs, moving wheels, animal reactions, particle celebration and smooth scenery transitions. Respect reduced-motion settings.

**Visual acceptance:** Screens feel recognisably close to the approved concepts; Seb and Sam are identifiable in the app, not only in promotional art; all headings are readable; tractor reads unmistakably as farm machinery.

## B. Rebuild “Deliver the Hay”

**Goal:** Load a trailer and make a delivery, rather than repeatedly tapping a counter.

- [ ] Scene has a tractor, visibly attached **empty trailer**, and a group of hay bales in the field.
- [ ] **Seb:** drag a bale onto the trailer's large drop target. Bales visibly snap into a sensible stack; misplaced drops return gently to their starting place with no penalty.
- [ ] **Sam:** tap a bale to have it animate onto the trailer (dragging is optional); do not require fine motor control.
- [ ] Three bales per round by default (never require more than five). Each produces a soft realistic handling sound and a visible trailer change.
- [ ] Once full, unlock **Deliver!**; tractor actually **travels forward** toward a barn or feeding station along a short animated route.
- [ ] Arrival shows bales being unloaded, animals being fed and a celebratory completion.
- [ ] Repeat with a small change (trailer colour, farm destination or bale positions).

**Acceptance:** Real drag gesture works on Android touch screens, tap fallback works for Sam, payload and trip animations are visibly meaningful, and delivery is clearly completed.

## C. Replace “Tractor Time” with “Get Ready to Drive”

**Goal:** Carry out a short, recognisable farm job in order.

- [ ] Show a complete, recognisable tractor rather than a single emoji sliding sideways.
- [ ] Five steps with pictorial controls: **1 Start engine → 2 Turn on amber beacon → 3 Attach trailer → 4 Open gate → 5 Drive into field.**
- [ ] Engine visibly vibrates and plays realistic starting/running audio; beacon flashes; trailer moves/couples at hitch; gate swings open; tractor moves **forward** relative to the field.
- [ ] Instructions are brief and can be replayed; current step glows/highlights. Controls that aren't active yet give gentle guidance.
- [ ] **Seb:** performs all five actions; **Sam:** can progress by selecting each highlighted large icon without following multi-sentence instructions.
- [ ] Add small variations on repeat (red/green tractor, different field and trailer job). No traffic, collision or precision driving challenge.

**Acceptance:** No backwards movement; every step causes a visible and audible change; tractor reaches a destination and the job ends in a clear celebration.

## D. Replace “Barn Peekaboo” with “Find the Farm Animals”

**Goal:** Search a scene and discover animals behind moveable obstacles.

- [ ] Rename activity to **Find the Farm Animals**. Use an actual **barn illustration** (roof, barn doors, stalls and hay storage), not a house.
- [ ] Three search scenes: **Barn**, **Field**, **Farmyard**. Move between scenes after completing the current one.
- [ ] In each scene hide **3 animals** behind interactive elements: barn doors, straw piles, hay bales, wheelbarrow, feed sacks, bushes, troughs or gates, as appropriate.
- [ ] Tap/drag an obstruction; object animates aside and reveals a hidden animal. Animal moves slightly and makes a genuine recorded sound.
- [ ] Always show a simple **three-picture find checklist**; found animals receive a green tick or become colourful portraits.
- [ ] Finding all three unlocks a short celebration and **Next Place**. Allow replay with shuffled animals and hiding places.
- [ ] **Sam:** easy, prominent objects with one-tap reveal; **Seb:** a few more plausible hiding places and optional simple verbal clues.
- [ ] A revealed animal cannot be counted twice; scenes never dead-end due to obstructed or invisible controls.

**Acceptance:** Three meaningfully different environments, real barn artwork, clear seek/reveal/complete loop, visible three-animal progress.

## E. Rework “Animal Sounds” into “Explore the Farm”

**Goal:** Non-quiz sensory discovery, distinct from Guess the Farm Sound.

- [ ] Rename to **Explore the Farm** or **Meet the Farm Animals**.
- [ ] Present a lively illustrated farm scene, with animals visible in sensible places; tapping an animal makes it react, move and produce its **actual sound**.
- [ ] Add gentle cause-and-effect interactions: feed chickens, brush a pony, wake the sleeping dog, find a duck at the pond, etc.
- [ ] Allow simple panning/switching between stable, pasture and farmyard; both kids can play with no correct/incorrect responses.
- [ ] Add visual discoveries, not a picture-choice guessing quiz. No answer buttons or fail states.

**Acceptance:** Discover-and-play mechanics, at least six independent interactive hotspots, meaningfully different from Guess the Farm Sound.

## F. Expand farm animals and machinery across the app

**Animals target:** cow, sheep, pig, chicken, **horse/pony, duck, goat, dog and cat**.

**Machinery target:** **tractor, combine harvester, digger/excavator and front loader/telehandler**; trailer and gate as interactive farm objects.

- [ ] Illustrations sized for interactive UI, with consistent perspective and style.
- [ ] Add **properly licensed real recordings**, preferably at least two variations per common animal or machine (only if a genuine suitable recording exists). No text-to-speech animal imitations.
- [ ] Expand Guess the Farm Sound with the new sounds and exactly **three distinct image choices** each round. Avoid near-identical choices for younger players.
- [ ] Spread new animals/machines naturally across Explore, Find Animals and Tractor tasks—not only in a new menu.
- [ ] Offer authentic combine harvesting and digger scooping animations/sounds as exploratory interactions, without inventing complicated new standalone games in this release.
- [ ] Keep sounds locally bundled; document source, creator and reuse licence for every new recording. Normalise volume to avoid surprising jumps and avoid very loud machinery clips.

**Acceptance:** New farm subjects are both audible and visibly interactive, and assets function offline.

## G. Existing games and continuity

- [ ] Retain **Count the Sheep** and **Tractor Colours**, and keep planting/growing. Improve scenery/button styling to match the new art direction.
- [ ] Sheep counting is **1 to 5 only**, including narration and option labels. Do not create any accidental numbers above five.
- [ ] Preserve Sam/Seb mode selection, back navigation, sound/music preferences and safety/privacy protections.
- [ ] Make game titles and icons match the rebuilt activities.

## Release definition of done

v1.4 is complete only when: (1) all eight activities remain available or have been intentionally renamed/rebuilt, (2) the headline graphics no longer collide and both boys are clearly visible, (3) Hay Delivery, Tractor Time and Find Animals have meaningful completion loops, (4) real recorded farm sounds support the expanded world, (5) all primary touch controls work for a toddler, (6) both age modes and the 1–5 counting limit are preserved, and (7) the full Android package builds and has been visually checked on a device.

**Out of scope for v1.4:** online accounts, ads, in-app purchases, complex levels, scores/leaderboards, multiplayer, GPS, or unrestricted driving simulations.
